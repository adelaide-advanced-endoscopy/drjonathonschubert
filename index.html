About Dr Schubert

Precision, expertise and a specialist focus.

Dr Jonathon Schubert is a Consultant Gastroenterologist and Interventional Endoscopist with a particular focus on advanced diagnostic and therapeutic endoscopy.

Following specialist training in Gastroenterology and Hepatology in South Australia, Dr Schubert completed an Interventional Endoscopy Fellowship at the Royal Brisbane and Women's Hospital. He subsequently undertook further training in flexible endoscopic surgery and ESD in leading centres in Germany and Japan.

His practice includes the assessment and endoscopic treatment of complex mucosal lesions, selected early gastrointestinal cancers, large colorectal polyps, Barrett's-related neoplasia and other conditions requiring advanced endoscopic expertise.

Dr Schubert works closely with referring gastroenterologists, General Practitioners, surgeons, oncologists and multidisciplinary teams to determine the most appropriate approach for each patient.

He also has a background in Engineering and a PhD from the University of Adelaide focused on Helicobacter pylori and gastric cancer risk.

For patients & referring doctors

Advanced endoscopy when a more specialised approach is needed.

Most patients do not need advanced endoscopy. For selected lesions, however, techniques such as EMR and ESD can allow removal of significant gastrointestinal lesions without conventional surgery. These procedures require careful assessment of the lesion, its depth and its cancer risk.

EMR

Endoscopic mucosal resection

Removal of selected larger or complex mucosal lesions using an endoscopic technique, commonly in the colon and upper gastrointestinal tract.

ESD

Endoscopic submucosal dissection

A highly specialised technique allowing en-bloc removal of selected superficial gastrointestinal neoplasia, including lesions where conventional polypectomy or EMR may not be suitable.

EUS

Endoscopic ultrasound

Specialist ultrasound performed from within the gastrointestinal tract, including selected tissue sampling and assessment of upper gastrointestinal and pancreaticobiliary conditions.

BALLOON ENTEROSCOPY

Small bowel endoscopy

Deep enteroscopy for selected small bowel lesions, bleeding and other conditions identified through capsule endoscopy or other investigations.

Common questions

Before you book.

Can I be referred specifically for ESD or EMR?

Yes. Patients with large or complex gastrointestinal lesions can be referred for an advanced endoscopic opinion. The most appropriate treatment is determined after review of the clinical history, endoscopic findings and pathology where available.

Is advanced endoscopy an alternative to surgery?

For selected early gastrointestinal neoplasia, endoscopic resection may provide definitive treatment while preserving the involved organ. Suitability depends on the depth and nature of the lesion, the risk of lymph-node or metastatic disease, technical considerations and the available alternatives.

What happens after an ESD or EMR?

The resection specimen is reviewed by pathology and the results are used to determine whether endoscopic surveillance, further endoscopic treatment or another treatment approach is appropriate. Recommendations are communicated to the patient and referring doctor.

Where are procedures performed?

Procedures are performed at selected accredited private hospitals and day hospitals in Adelaide. The appropriate location depends on the procedure and the patient's individual requirements.
